# Axis email system

The information is free. The paid offer is implementation, personalization, and time saved.

## Non-negotiable sender rule

Every message comes from **Bradley**. STAN, CUSTOMER_SERVICE, BRIDGE, OMNI, and Axis are internal operating roles only; none may impersonate the sender.

Every sales email must:

- use one verified, specific observation;
- sound decisive without overstating results;
- ask no more than two questions;
- include the 15-minute booking path: https://cal.com/bradley-dennis-ddfihy/15min?overlayCalendar=true
- sign “— Bradley” followed by “Axis”;
- include a plain-language opt-out;
- avoid guaranteed leads, revenue, rankings, or outcomes.

## Inbound audit nurture

### Email 1 — Confirmation
**Subject:** [First name], your Axis website audit is in Bradley’s queue

Confirm receipt, set delivery expectations, and ask for the recipient’s primary website goal.

### Email 2 — Audit delivery
**Subject:** [Business]: the three fixes I’d make first

Deliver the audit. Summarize the three highest-impact observed issues before mentioning implementation.

### Email 3 — The cost of unclear messaging
Explain how unclear positioning can cause qualified visitors to leave. Show one concrete fix and ask one diagnostic question.

### Email 4 — The conversion path
Explain the role of a clear CTA, proof, and reduced friction. Invite a reply.

### Email 5 — Before-and-after breakdown
Show a real redesign or concept build. Explain each decision instead of showing aesthetics alone.

### Email 6 — Implementation options
Present the smallest scope that solves the diagnosed problem. Explain why a smaller scope under-solves and a larger scope overbuilds.

### Email 7 — Direct decision
**Subject:** Fix it now, revisit later, or leave it alone?

Offer three honest choices and one reply CTA. No fake scarcity.

## Experiment rules

Keep each lead’s assigned arm sticky:

- **STAN:** decisive diagnosis and first fix.
- **CUSTOMER_SERVICE:** listening-first, rapport, and one practical question.
- **BRIDGE:** equal parts authority and warmth.

Judge arms on replies, qualified conversations, bookings, and revenue per delivered email. Do not choose a winner from opens alone.
